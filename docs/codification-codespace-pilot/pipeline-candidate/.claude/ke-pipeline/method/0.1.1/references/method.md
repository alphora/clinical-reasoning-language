# Shared medical-policy modeling method

Read with the release's CRL contract map. Deployment and behavioral acceptance remain separately recorded.

## Authority and session entry

Source fidelity is the highest priority: represent the authorized source correctly, completely and accurately. Improve human inspectability and question relevance within that constraint. Preserve faithful logic when a tool or client cannot provide the intended experience, and report the specific gap.

Keep three responsibilities separate:

| Authority | Owns |
|---|---|
| CRL kit matched to the accepted actual tools/runtime, and verified client contracts | Supported language forms, publication and answer semantics, dependencies, execution behavior, diagnostics and technical limitations |
| Shared method | Source analysis, logical decomposition, question design, independent review and evidence discipline |
| Applicable customer, source, deployment and lifecycle profiles | Authorized source scope and rulings, outcome vocabulary and meaning, presentation conventions, client obligations, producer roles, blind-authoring boundaries and promotion procedures |

**At a new session or resumption, reload the accepted method, its CRL contract map, applicable customer/source/lifecycle profiles and active task record. At the KE reference/workflow stage, retrieve the kit matched to the accepted actual tools/runtime and verify its capabilities before semantic reliance.** Reconcile the active task, source and candidate identities, scoped decisions, authorization and unfinished evidence. Record what was actually loaded and verified. This is an instruction to perform those steps; file presence, installation or a previous session does not prove automatic loading or current adoption.

Respect each mapped kit clause's actual force: defaults, invariants and validator-enforced rules are distinct. A faithful alternative may override a default. The [contract map](crl-contract-map.md) binds CRL-derived claims to exact clauses and an assessed kit identity; it also identifies KE-method practices with no invented kit force. On kit hash or accepted version changes, perform targeted semantic reassessment of the mapped clauses and in-play dependencies during KE reference/workflow work, recording findings in the existing task/review record. The read-only SessionStart loader verifies every manifest-listed installed-default file and injects the available-default SKILL bootstrap and paths. It does not resolve the project binding, read customer profiles, or inject the full references. The agent subsequently resolves the actual binding and active task, loads the accepted release and applicable profiles, and reads the full method and contract map. The loader performs no network retrieval, semantic reassessment or acceptance-state mutation. Old examples and the newest kit elsewhere do not establish compatibility with the accepted tools/runtime. Unsettled language capability belongs with its tool owner; unresolved source meaning belongs with its authorized source or customer decision owner.

## Establish the source model

Read the complete supplied source and its refinement, including qualifications, exclusions and explanatory material. Check their correspondence before accepting model attribution. A reference does not automatically incorporate another document. Route missing necessary material to the responsible producer; add it only within explicit source authority. Neither a prior encoding nor its historical review can legitimize unsupported additions.

Before choosing constructs, state the requested determination, scope, source-defined scenarios and outcomes, applicability, independent obligations, complete alternatives and genuine precedence. Identify shared facts and meaningful assessed conditions. Map each requirement to its intended representation, including entity identity, timing, causal relationships and paired evidence. Explain necessary implementation glue separately from source requirements.

Treat the source and applicable scoped rulings as the acceptance oracle. Interpret ambiguities only within their actual authority. Do not silently repair contradictions, replace stated requirements with preferred practice, or assume an old model is correct. Preserve faithful surrounding structure during focused corrections. For source refresh or migration, follow the applicable isolation and lifecycle profile, adjudicating every behavior change against the source.

Specify qualifying, failing, alternative, overlapping and incomplete expectations before observing model predictions. Distinguish unanswered evidence, explicit false, not applicable, absent records and execution errors. Missing evidence does not establish a documented negative. Preserve source eligibility and source-defined precedence, then establish partial-answer expectations under the matched, accepted CRL/client contracts. Preserve any source-defined incomplete-request meaning and report a contract conflict instead of weakening it. Verify the effect of grouping, sequencing or factoring on those expectations, including errors.

## Give each construct a clear responsibility

| Need | Modeling responsibility |
|---|---|
| One meaningful answerable datum | A concept with an appropriate typed answer or supported source/computation; separate identity, answer vocabulary, classification and question wording |
| A coherent Boolean condition assessed as a unit, or the same condition genuinely reused | A Criterion with a transparent body and complete dependencies; meaningful single use can be a faithful override of the mapped one-use-inline default |
| Relationships among co-equal conditions | Inline Boolean grouping or a meaningful Criterion, preserving AND/OR/NOT relationships and complete alternatives |
| Applicability, progression, routing or consequences | Decision branches with width and depth earned by those relationships |
| A separately requested or genuinely shared determination | Supported decision delegation with preserved scope and consequences |
| Repeated remaining action-bearing structure reached from real caller paths | A supported continuation, after verifying that factoring preserves behavior and invocation scope |
| Source-intended multiple consequences | The current supported action composition, with verified multiplicity and completion semantics |

Logical grouping is the primary value of a Criterion; its name labels that structure. Reuse or a coherent assessed condition can justify the group. For the mapped kit, keeping one-use conditions inline remains the shipped default. This method's justified coherent-single-use practice is a faithful override of that default, not a changed kit rule. Cosmetic wrappers, a whole-source label hiding unrelated obligations, or manufactured callers do not. A Criterion is not an extra respondent attestation or an assumed question-scheduling boundary.

Separate inference from composition. Supported representations may provide evidence of one underlying clinical event or state. Distinct source criteria belong in transparent Boolean decision or Criterion structure. Do not invent inference concepts or defined-as composites that hide distinct clinical events under one label. Compound guards and transparent named Criteria are supported ways to retain those distinct operands and dependencies; a tree node for every atom is not required. Neither emitted condition-entry counts nor a mechanical guard-atom check replaces reading the source and the actual expressions.

Decision width and depth can make reasoning understandable. Inline Boolean nesting can make relationships within a condition understandable. Choose from source meaning and the assessed interview contract, without prescribing a flat model, a deep tree, a layer count or a construct quota. Do not turn independent conjuncts into arbitrary prerequisites or independent alternatives into ordered competitors. Keep each alternative's qualifiers and same-instance relationships together.

Retrieve the current kit rules for every selected form and its dependencies. Verify supported composition rather than inferring it from underlying implementation capabilities. Factoring must preserve entry conditions, partial-answer and error behavior, output identity and multiplicity, and relevant question scope. Check the intended invocation root and preserve provenance through structural changes.

For coverage/authorization determinations, apply the mapped communicated-not-ordered contract: communicate the determination rather than order a service. Verify exactly one determination on successful completion and none during a needed-unknown pause across the full delegated closure and sibling fanout. Preserve configured membership and finality-by-mode; flavor names and meanings remain local. This scoped determination rule does not prohibit source-intended multiple nondetermination actions.

## Design the questions and the client interview

Ask for meaningful evidence at a grain a respondent can answer faithfully. Preserve distinct obligations and required relationships without splitting every phrase into a separate question. A categorical selection must retain the source's cardinality and evidence pairings; one answer cannot stand in for facts that must coexist. Avoid unsupported whole-policy judgments and do not assume document-content inspection or upstream data availability.

For each representative route, explain why an input is required, whether it is already supplied, when it becomes inapplicable and whether its answer can still affect the intended assessment. Distinguish source-required precedence from an author-chosen interview sequence. An interview choice must not silently change independent eligibility logic.

Establish expected question identities and partial-answer behavior before execution. Test actual native and target-client behavior after answers, changes and clears, retaining relevant evidence and correction access. Distinguish inputs in emitted definitions, returned form items, retained answers and questions the client requires next.

For a successfully settled assessment, required interview progression should stop for inputs that no longer serve that assessment or an authorized evidence purpose. The form and answers may remain available for review or correction; changes or clears can reopen the assessment. An activity alone does not prove completion when errors, partial multi-action output or another required assessment remain. Observe the target client's stopping and reopening behavior. Raw item retention establishes neither a defect nor satisfactory interaction.

A named condition, a shorter demonstration or a correct terminal result does not prove question suppression. Preserve faithful logic if the runtime or client cannot meet the scoped interview contract; record the unresolved requirement and responsible owner.

## Independent review and evidence

Reviewers independently reconstruct source requirements before accepting the author's map. Keep authoring, lead adjudication and independent execution responsibilities distinct under the review profile. Report the actual roles, instructions loaded, capabilities and unavailable coverage; a read-only opinion is not execution evidence.

Give separate findings for source/behavior fidelity, human inspectability and question relevance. Passing one does not establish the others. Review the actual source map, decision and Criterion bodies, emitted expressions and dependencies, and rendered decision surface bound to the candidate revision.

A human read-back must explain a qualifying route and a failing or alternative route in source language, expand meaningful groups, and locate the exact expression, logical relationship, affected paths and unchanged paths for a realistic maintenance amendment. Inspect group boundaries and their connections, not labels alone. A structural export can support analysis when rendering is unavailable, but rendered inspectability then remains unverified. An independently redrawn illustration does not verify the delivered viewing surface.

Independent expectations must be falsifiable and capable of detecting lost requirements, mixed-instance evidence, altered precedence and changed unknown behavior. Bind expectations to exact typed inputs and evaluation state. A negative case needs actual disqualifying data; omission belongs in a separate missing-input case with its own expected state. Compare predictive results with computed native results and investigate divergence; do not generalize one predictor's omission behavior across all forms or substitute its prediction for native evidence. Check exact outputs, multiplicity and paths where applicable; output membership alone is insufficient. Do not change an oracle to match a prediction or invent output variants to simplify proof.

Human medical-validation examples are a deliberate, representative review set, distinct from the fuller regression corpus. Maintain the latter's relevant negative, partial-input, overlap, boundary and error coverage; do not turn every regression case into a human-review example or treat a short review set as exhaustive proof.

Keep validation, emission, predictive execution, native execution, rendered inspection, client interaction and source fidelity as distinct evidence. Capture exact tool inputs, results, diagnostics, relevant traces and raw form/response exchanges. Verify resource identities, references, configuration and stale extras. Record failed, unsupported and unexecuted checks separately. Hashes identify bytes; they do not prove a claimed run occurred or that a model is faithful.

Maintain source-first attribution and correspondence across refinements and structural changes. Preserve stable case identity where the case is unchanged, explicitly map changed cases, and retain human concerns and review history across renames. Inspect waivers and unchecked cases rather than treating a mechanical pass as coverage of every axis.

The lead dispositions each finding with a reason, verifies warranted corrections, and records unresolved source, artifact, tool and client issues with their scope and owners. A review recommendation is not automatic clinical certification or authorization to publish. Human acceptance and lifecycle attestations remain with the configured producers.

## Required local bindings

Each installation must declare its maintained release location, version, update route and session-entry integration. Each customer/source/lifecycle profile owner provides current scoped authority, source boundaries, outcome contracts, review roles and promotion requirements. The CRL tool owner supplies current kit and capability evidence. Deployment and client owners identify the actual rendering surface and interview contract, including completion, retained forms and reopening. Existing task and review records carry accepted guidance, source correspondence, execution evidence and unresolved concerns.

Adoption requires those concrete bindings and evidence that the method and profiles were loaded in the relevant sessions. A distribution change by itself does not establish implementation, runtime behavior or clinical readiness.
