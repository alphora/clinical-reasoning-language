The mapping below distinguishes:

- **V — verified bounded mapping:** the cited assertions support the stated observation. This is not a new execution pass.
- **M — manual review:** intent, clinical fidelity, workflow authorization or deployment judgment.
- **G — gap/limitation:** an unsupported capability or missing exact behavioral evidence.
- **H — historical/optional native evidence:** tied to its recorded fixture, engine and invocation; not current637 execution.
